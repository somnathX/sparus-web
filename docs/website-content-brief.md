# Sparus Technology

## What we are

Sparus Technology is a product-minded engineering company. We build software, integrate AI where it actually helps, and set up the infrastructure underneath so things work reliably in production—not just in demos.

We work with scale-ups modernizing their stack, enterprises dealing with legacy systems, and teams that need senior engineers without hiring a full department overnight.

## What we do

**Software development** — Apps, APIs, and internal tools. We focus on tests, observability, and documentation so your team can own the code long-term.

**AI integration** — Retrieval, agents, and ML pipelines tied to your data and compliance needs. Includes evaluation, safety, and rollback—not just slideware.

**Infrastructure & DevOps** — Cloud, containers, CI/CD, and automation. Cost-aware, observable, and built for real incidents at 3 a.m.

## Who we work with

- **High-growth teams** — Ship fast without breaking architecture every six months
- **Enterprises modernizing** — Gradual migrations, ERP/CRM integrations, no big-bang outages
- **Regulated environments** — Security, logging, access controls, and audit-friendly practices

## How we work

- Based in **Udaipur, India** (HQ) with a regional office in **Dubai, UAE**
- Work with clients remotely worldwide
- Schedule overlap across time zones for stand-ups, reviews, and incidents
- Every engagement should end with code, runbooks, and context your team can inherit
- Direct communication with people who write and review code—no black-box delivery pods

## Our values

- **Outcomes over theater** — Shipped value, uptime, and flexibility—not vanity metrics
- **Direct lines** — You talk to engineers, not layers of account managers
- **Pace you can sustain** — Automation and docs so you're not paying forever for one heroic launch
